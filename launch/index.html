How it works

Four steps from sign-up to game day.

  1. Submit your team using the form below.
  2. MPO reviews your entry and contacts you to confirm your slot.
  3. Pay the registration fee. Slots go to the first teams to pay.
  4. Show up at Balai Mindoro and play.

Team registration
