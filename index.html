How it works

Four steps from sign-up to game day.

  1. Submit Registration: One player fills out the online form for the team.
  2. Wait for Review: MPO reviews your team's skill level and category within 3 to 5 days (Open Advance category skips this step).
  3. Get Approved: Once approved, MPO will send you the payment details.
  4. Pay & Confirm: Pay the registration fee and upload your proof of payment to secure your confirmed slot.

Team registration

Enter both players' details. Submitting does not hold a slot until MPO confirms it and you pay.
